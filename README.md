💌 Love Letter – An Interactive Digital Proposal

A small project made with HTML, CSS & JavaScript to turn a simple proposal into something a little more special. ❤️

This isn’t just a normal webpage. It’s an interactive digital love letter designed to create a small experience for someone special. From opening the letter to going through the little interactions, the whole idea is to make the proposal feel personal, memorable, and fun.

I originally made this project as a way to propose to someone special, but I thought it would be even better if anyone could use the idea for their own special person. So if you’re looking for a cute and different way to ask your crush, girlfriend, boyfriend, or someone you love, you can use this project and make it your own. 🫶🏻

💗 Want to try it?

👉 https://hitecker.github.io/Love-letter/

Open the link, experience the full interactive love letter, and if you like the idea, you can take the project and customize it for your own story.

You can change the names, messages, dates, questions, and other details to make it personal. You can also modify the design and interactions according to your own idea.

✨ Made for people who want to say “I like you” in a slightly more memorable way.

If you use this project to propose to your crush, I hope they say YES. ❤️
